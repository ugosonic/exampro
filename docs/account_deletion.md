# ZenovTech - Citizen Test Account Deletion

This page applies to **Citizen Test** (also referenced as **Citizenship Test**) by **ZenovTech**.

## How to request account deletion

Send an email to **info@zenovtech.com** with the subject:

`Account Deletion Request - Citizen Test`

Please include:
- The email address used to sign in.
- The country test category you selected (e.g., UK, US, Canada, Australia).
- A short confirmation that you want your account deleted.

## What data will be deleted

When we verify your request, we delete:
- Your account record (email and authentication data).
- Any server-stored exam attempts or progress linked to your account (if synced).
- Server-side preferences linked to your account (if any).

## What data may be kept

We may retain:
- Minimal security logs needed to protect the service and prevent abuse.
- Records required by law or to resolve disputes.
- Google Play purchase records are stored by Google, not by ZenovTech.

## Retention period

Deletion requests are processed within **30 days** after verification.
Any retained security or legal logs are kept for up to **90 days** unless a longer period is required by law.

## Partial deletion (optional)

At this time, we do **not** support deleting specific data while keeping an active account.
If this changes, we will update this page.
