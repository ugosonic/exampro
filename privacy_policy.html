This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Citizenship Test mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1) Information We Collect

A. Information you provide

  • Account information: When you create an account or sign in, we collect information needed to authenticate you, such as email address and password.
  • Support communications (if you contact us): If you contact support, we may collect your email address and the content of your message.
  • Payments (if you choose to upgrade): If you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed by payment providers (for example, Stripe, and where available Google Pay / Apple Pay). Payment details (such as card number) are collected directly by the payment provider in their payment UI. We receive limited transaction details (such as purchase status and transaction identifiers) needed to grant access and support purchases.

B. Information stored on your device

  • Learning and progress data: The App stores your learning progress locally on your device, such as quiz attempts, answers, scores, completion status, preferences, and settings.
  • Notification settings: If you enable reminders, the App stores notification-related settings locally on your device.

C. Information collected automatically

  • Device or other IDs (push notifications): If push notifications are enabled, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM may process device or other identifiers such as an FCM registration token and Firebase installation identifiers in order to deliver notifications.
  • App activity (sync): If you sign in and use sync, we may transmit and store progress-related data on our servers (for example: quiz attempts, answers, scores, and saved questions) to support backup and cross-device access.

D. Information we do not collect

  • We do not run third-party ads in the App.

2) How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and manage your account and enable sign-in.
  • Provide App functionality (including saving your progress and, if enabled, syncing it across devices).
  • Respond to support requests if you contact us.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App (for example, preventing unauthorized access).
  • Deliver notifications and reminders (if you enable notifications).
  • Process upgrades and provide access to paid features (if you choose to purchase).

3) Payments

The App may be offered as a paid app on Google Play. Payment is handled by Google Play before download.

We do not collect or store your payment card details for Google Play purchases. Google Play may provide us limited purchase-related information (such as confirmation that you have access to the paid download) to enable access to the App.

If the App offers an in-app upgrade (for example, Pro features), payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers (such as Stripe). In those cases, the payment provider collects payment information directly, and we receive limited transaction details needed to grant access (for example, a payment/transaction identifier and status).

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Services, for example:
